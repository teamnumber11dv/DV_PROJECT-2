⚽ Football Match Analysis Dashboard

StatsBomb Event-Level Data Visualization | Team-11 Project

1. Turnover Locations and Structural Vulnerabilities

This grid-based heatmap shows where teams lose possession. A purple sequential scale highlights high-turnover zones, while individual circles show each turnover event, color-coded by type. You can optionally overlay tactical zones (Zone 14 and half-spaces) and see zone details on hover for deeper tactical context.

2. Shot Origin and Attacking Biases

Explore where teams generate shooting opportunities and identify spatial biases in attacking approach. Symbols are sized by xG (expected goals), with goals emphasised using a distinct star shape and a subtle jitter applied to overlapping locations so repeated shots from similar spots remain visible.

3. Defensive Actions by Zone

Pitch is divided into three defensive zones (Defensive, Middle, Attacking thirds). Each defensive action is represented with an intuitive icon (e.g., 🥾 for tackles, 🛡️ for blocks). Successful actions use solid fills, while failed ones are shown as hollow circles so success rate is legible even without zooming. Hover over icons for details and use the filters and legend to reduce clutter.
